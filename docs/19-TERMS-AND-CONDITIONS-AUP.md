Only one Workspace should be registered per legitimate company/organization. Registering multiple Workspaces to abuse free-tier limits (e.g., to exceed the 5-member Free Starter Pilot cap by splitting one team across several Workspaces) is not permitted.

### 1.2 Invite-Only Team Membership
- Team members join a Workspace **exclusively** through a valid invite from an Admin or Manager of that Workspace.
- Sharing your personal invite link or account credentials with someone else to gain unauthorized access is prohibited.
- Attempting to guess, forge, or reuse an expired/already-used invite token is prohibited and may be treated as an attempted security breach.

### 1.3 Role Conduct
- **Admins** are responsible for accurately assigning roles (Admin/Manager/Employee) based on actual organizational authority — assigning Admin/Manager roles to gain unauthorized system access for someone is a violation.

- **Employees** must not attempt to access data, dashboards, or actions reserved for Manager/Admin roles, including by circumventing the application's role-based routing.
